= START
# PotentialBleed: ghost bleed
Listens for each hit and adds quiet, synthetic residue behind it: snare wires buzzing, a cabinet booming, a piano board ringing. Your chops sound like they came off a real recording, with other instruments leaking in.

- Add it as an insert effect on a chopped loop, a drum bus, a track or a pad. It is not in the instrument list.
- Quick start: turn LISTEN on. Lower SENSE until the important hits ring, then raise BLEED until you hear it clearly.
- Turn LISTEN off and bring BLEED back down until the residue sits behind the track.
- Nothing is delayed, so your MPC's timing stays exactly where it was.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= CONTROLS
# Controls
- SOURCE: what leaks in (next page). BLEED: how much. Small amounts usually sound most real.
- SENSE: how hard a hit has to be, -60 to -6 dB.
- CHANCE: how often a hit leaks. 100 % is every hit, lower values give the odd ghost.
- DECAY: how long the residue rings, 30 ms to 1.5 s.
- DUCK keeps it down under loud audio. GAPS brings it up in the quiet spaces.
- TONE: dark to bright. TICK: adds a needle tick on each hit.
- DRIFT: a slow wobble, slightly different left and right, on the whole sound.
- LISTEN: hear just the residue, to set it up. Turn it off again for the mix.
- ROLL: picks a new source and shape. MILD, MEDIUM or WILD sets how far it goes.

= SOURCES
# Sources and presets
All eight sources are generated in the plugin (tuned resonances and filtered noise), not recordings.

- WIRES: snare wires rattling behind the kick. CABINET: a bass cabinet's low thump and boom.
- SOUNDBOARD: a piano's wooden board ringing. TINES: bright struck tines, electric-piano style.
- STRINGS: string scrape and ring. CYMBAL: a soft metallic wash.
- ROOM: a small room's low ring and air. BARS: vibraphone-like bars.

MPC's PRESET menu: Wires behind the kit, Bass in the room, Piano lid open, Rhodes shimmer, Guitar scrape, Cymbal wash, Small room, Vibes and Old record, then the four chain presets.

= CHAINS
# FX chains
Put the plugins on a track's inserts in the order shown, then pick the same preset name in each one.

- DUSTY LOOP (soul, jazz and library loops): BLEED > Patina > Grit. Bleed is on SOUNDBOARD.
- 90s STAB (chopped horns, keys and vocal stabs): Grit > Punch > BLEED > Patina. Bleed is on TINES.
- BREAK BUS (a boom-bap drum bus): Punch > Grit > BLEED > Patina. Bleed is on WIRES.
- DUB DRUMS (lo-fi dub drums): Patina > Punch > BLEED > Grit. Bleed is on ROOM.

Here, in Patina and in Grit the name is in the MPC's PRESET menu. In Punch it is on the PRESETS buttons.

Rules of thumb: Punch early, Bleed before Patina so the bleed is aged with the sound, echo and chop last, one noise source per chain, and match levels with OUTPUT before you decide.

= USER
# User presets
- To save: open the USER page, tap SAVE, then tap a slot. The slot now reads USER and its number.
- To load: tap a slot. The slot you loaded last is lit.
- Saving to a slot that is already filled replaces it straight away.
- The 8 slots are kept on the drive the plugin is on, in a folder called PotentialSuite Presets. Every project and every copy of the plugin sees them, and updating the plugin keeps them.
- A slot keeps the knobs and the source. It does not keep LISTEN, the ROLL setting or the look.
- A chain of your own: save each plugin's part to the same slot number, for example USER 3 in Punch, Grit and Bleed. Later, tap USER 3 in each.

= HELP
# Good to know
- Q-Links: BLEED, SOURCE, DECAY, SENSE, CHANCE, DUCK, GAPS and TONE, then TICK, DRIFT, OUTPUT and LISTEN.
- Every setting is saved with your project.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack or Colour Block, on the SETTINGS page. A new look shows the next time the plugin loads.
- There is no lookahead and nothing is delayed.
- If nothing rings, lower SENSE. If everything rings, raise it.
- Stereo in, stereo out, at 44.1 kHz.
